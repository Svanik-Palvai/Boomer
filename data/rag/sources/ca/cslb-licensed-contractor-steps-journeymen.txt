CONTRACTORS STATE LICENSE BOARD
Steps to Becoming a Licensed Contractor



Any construction project in California priced at $1,000 or
more in labor and materials combined must be performed
by a contractor with a Contractors State License Board
(CSLB) issued license. Any project needing a permit or
workers requires a contractor's license.
