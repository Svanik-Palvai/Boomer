 Executive Directive 19: NEW Service and Construction Standards 

To help developers move more efficiently through the design and review pipeline, LADWP is modernizing processes and systems to reduce delays and improve communication.

Learn more about how LADWP’s ED19 efforts are helping streamline service connections and supporting homes, businesses, and the future infrastructure of Los Angeles at LADWP.com/ED19.

Standards Deployment Notice

As LADWP implements improvements to standards in support of Executive Directive 19, the current version of documents, drawings, and specifications posted on this site may be subject to revisions. For the latest information or questions regarding specific requirements, please contact your LADWP service planning representative.

Standard Design Templates

LADWP has implemented a series of new standardized design templates called “Standard Plans” to assist customers by significantly improving the design timeline for projects that can accommodate a simplified standardized design.  Customers can consider incorporating these designs in the development phase of their projects to significantly improve the LADWP design and approval process for new service requests. Consult with your LADWP Service Planner for more information and application.

Construction Work Area

The document defines technical clearance and access requirements for pad mounted equipment, including an unobstructed 18 by 22 foot work zone, 30 foot monument separation, and minimum 22 foot alley width, with private property space required when alley access is insufficient; these standards supplement applicable P721 00 criteria and are in addition to the existing staging area. Consult with your LADWP Service Planning for more information and application.
