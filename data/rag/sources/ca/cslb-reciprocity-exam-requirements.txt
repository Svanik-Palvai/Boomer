Reciprocity Exam Requirements

If you hold a valid contractors license in Arizona, Louisiana, Mississippi, Nevada or North Carolina, and meet the requirements for reciprocity, you may be able to get a California contractors license without taking the trade portion of the exam.

 REMEMBER - CSLB may waive the trade portion of the exam but retains the right to require the trade exam.

 IMPORTANT - If the applicant is granted reciprocity, they still will be required to take the business law portion of CSLB's exam.
