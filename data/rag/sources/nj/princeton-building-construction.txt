About Us
The Building Construction Department ensures the safety and compliance of buildings, homes, and renovations in Princeton. We review plans, issue permits, and conduct inspections to protect public safety under New Jersey Uniform Construction Code (NJUCC) — the state-mandated code that ensures the safety and well-being of Princeton’s residents. The department also applies the International Building Code (IBC) and International Residential Code (IRC), New Jersey Editions, along with the relevant codes adopted by the State of New Jersey and Princeton Council. These include the International Code Council’s Mechanical, Plumbing, and Fuel Gas Codes, the National Electrical Code (NFPA 70), other NFPA fire codes, and the New Jersey Uniform Fire Code. Our mission is to safeguard the public by ensuring that all construction in Princeton meets rigorous safety and compliance standards.

 Inspections

Inspections
Licensed state inspectors enforce compliance with the NJUCC, IBC, IRC, and all applicable codes.
Inspections cover building, plumbing, electrical, mechanical, and fire safety systems.
Codes enforced include:
International Mechanical, Plumbing, and Fuel Gas Codes.
National Electrical Code (NFPA 70).
Uniform Fire Code (NJ Edition).
NFPA Fire Codes.
The NJUCC stipulates that all inspections are to be completed and approved prior to final payments being made.

 Department Functions

Department Functions
Enforce the use of the state-approved equipment, and its installation, requirements
Inspect all work that homeowners or contractors do, to ensure maximum safety. 
Review all submitted plans for compliance with building and safety codes.
