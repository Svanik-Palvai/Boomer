              UCC SUMMARY OF RULE CHANGES – SUMMER      2026 UPDATE


August 17, 2026, New Jersey Register

N.J.A.C. 5:23-  3.14, 3.15, 3.16, 3.17, 3.18, 3.20, 3.21, and 3.22     – Subcodes – This adoption updates the
building subcode, plumbing subcode, electrical subcode, fire protection subcode, energy subcode,
mechanical subcode, one-   and two-family dwelling subcode and fuel gas subcode     within the Uniform
Construction Code   to the applicable 2024 International Code Council model codes, 2024 National
Standard Plumbing Code, and the 2023 National Electrical Code.
          for more information, please see “Oct 20, 2025          ” row at
         https://www.nj.gov/dca/codes/codreg/rule_proposals_adoptions.shtml

N.J.A.C. 5:23-  2.18, 2.34, 2.35, 3.4, 3.8, 5.3, 6.2, 6.3A, 6.4, 6.5, 6.6, 6.7, 6.8, 6.9, 6.12A, 6.13A, 6.14A,
6.15A, 6.16A, 6.17A, 6.18A, 6.19A, 6.20A, 6.21A, 6.22A, 6.23A, 6.24A, 6.25A, 6.26A, 6.28A, 6.31, and
6.32     – Rehabilitation Subcode, Responsibilities, and Rest of UCC – This adoption updates the
rehabilitation subcode, subcode responsibilities, and other UCC items to coincide with the adoption of the
latest cycle of model codes.
          for more information, please see “Oct 20, 2025      ” row at
         https://www.nj.gov/dca/codes/codreg/rule_proposals_adoptions.shtml

N.J.A.C. 5:23-  2.15, 2.15A, 2.16, and 4.4 – Administration and Enforcement; Process and Enforcing
Agencies; Duties; Powers; Procedures – This adoption amends Subchapters 2 and 4 of the Uniform
Construction Code to recognize master hearth specialists, address internal inconsistencies within the
single-  family dwelling permitting process, incorporate an omitted provision from P.L. 2022, c.139, and
assign a number to the form submitted pursuant to N.J.A.C. 5:23-    4.4.
          for more information, please see “Sep 02, 2025          ” row at
         https://www.nj.gov/dca/codes/codreg/rule_proposals_adoptions.shtml
