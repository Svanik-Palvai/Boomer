                                                                                      Electric Vehicle Chargers
                                                                                                         Residential Permitting




Purpose:  Establish the process for the review and permit of Electric Vehicle Supply
Equipment (EVSE) in new and existing residential applications   .


     •     Existing Homes with EVSE installation

                  EVSE equipment installations can be reviewed and permitted over the counter if
                    the existing electrical service entrance section does not exceed 200-amp 1
                    phase and does not require an upgrade to facilitate the installation of the new
                    equipment.

                  EVSE equipment installations that require an electrical service entrance be
                    upgraded beyond 200-  amp 1 phase will be required to be formally submitted for
                    plan review.


     •     New Custom or Standard Single-  Family Homes with EVSE installation

                  EVSE equipment installations will be reviewed along with the building plans for
                    custom homes.

                  EVSE equipment installations will be reviewed along with the building plans for
                    standard homes (homebuilder plans for new subdivisions/tract homes).  Standard
                    plans only need to be reviewed one time and they can be used in multiple
                    locations by the builder.

                           EVSE equipment can be added to any existing standard plan by
                              submitting a revision to the original approved plan.

     •     Plans for review may be submitted in person by making an     appointment online at
          Planning   and Development Online Appointment Scheduling or electronically at the
          Shape PHX Customer Portal.

     •     See current plan review times at Plan Reviews: Turnaround Times .
