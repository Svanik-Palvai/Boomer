 Building Construction Codes

 Building codes are published to meet the needs of local jurisdictions charged with enforcement of building standards for construction and are updated to reflect the latest standards.

 On June 18, 2025, the Phoenix City Council approved the adoption of the 2024 Phoenix Building Construction Code (PBCC). The City of Phoenix is transitioning to this updated code with an effective date of August 1, 2025.  To view implementation guidelines, please find the link here. 2024 Building Code Implementation [/content/dam/phoenix/pddsite/documents/codes-ordinances/amendmentcodes/2024-building-code-implementation.pdf]

 To view a history of Phoenix's past code adoptions please visit Phoenix Construction Code history [/content/dam/phoenix/pddsite/documents/trt/external/dsd_trt_pdf_00550.pdf]

 NOTICE: Re: 2023 NEC 210.8(F) Exception 2 - To ensure adequate product availability prior to mandatory enforcement, the city of Phoenix is deferring enforcement at this time. Full enforcement of the GFCI requirement for HVAC equipment will now take effect on March 1 st , 2027.

 2024 City of Phoenix Building Construction Codes
