                2024 Phoenix Building Construction Code Implementation

On June 18th,   2025, the City of Phoenix adopted the 2024     Phoenix Building
Construction Code   (PBCC). The 2024     PBCC has       an effective date of August 1, 2025.

Plans submitted for an initial   review beginning August  1, 2025, must comply with the
newly adopted 2024 PBCC and associated amendments, with the following exceptions
noted below.

After August 1, 2025,  the 2018     PBCC will remain in effect for complete  plans submitted
through December 31, 2025, for the following situations:

    •     Building plans for projects currently in the development review process – Must
        obtain approval from the PDD T eam Leader or Deputy Director

    •     Building plans submitted prior to August    1, 2025     – Subsequent plan review
        submittals can remain under the 2018 PBCC and permits can be           purchased
        within one year of approval of the plans

    •     Other plans and permits on a case   -by-case basis as allowed by the Building
        Official or Deputy Director

All plans submitted after December 31, 2025, must comply with the 2024 PBCC.

Standard plans, as defined by the PBCC, approved under the 2018     Phoenix Building
Construction Code will remain in effect until July 3 1, 2026.

For additional information, and to view the new codes and amendments, please visit our
webpage at: https://www.phoenix.gov/pdd/devcode/buildingcode
