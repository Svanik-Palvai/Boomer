State Fire Marshal: Eugene Zahara

Memorandum

To: Licensed Contractors, Businesses, Engineers, Architects, Certified Plans Examiners, Fire and Life Safety Inspectors, and Authorities Having Jurisdiction
From: The Office of the Nevada State Fire Marshal

After auditing the Fire Protection Engineering Bureau for the Nevada State Fire Marshal's Office significant violations were found. Businesses and/or contractors beginning work without permits issued by the Nevada State Fire Marshal's Office. Businesses and/or contractors concealing work prior to inspection by the Nevada State Fire Marshal's Office. All licensed fire and life safety companies, contractors, engineers, and architects should understand NAC 477, NRS 477, Codes and Standards adopted by the State, and the Nevada State Fire Marshal's Authority for fire plan review and fire and life safety inspections throughout the State. Businesses, contractors, engineers, and architects submitting to the Nevada State Fire Marshal's Office for plan review must cite current adopted code and standards by the Fire Marshal's Office.

Moving forward to resolve these issues the Nevada State Fire Marshal's Office will use all authority as outlined in NAC 477, NRS 477, 2024 International Fire Code, and adopted standards to enforce these violations. Enforcement will include but is not limited to: Notice of Violation as outlined in 2024 IFC 113.3, Stop Work Orders as outlined in 2024 IFC Section 114, Administrative Citation as outlined in NRS 477.240 with criminal penalty for violation of chapter; each day of violation constitutes separate offense as outlined in NRS 477.250. Administrative fines as outlined in NAC 477.991.

Licensed businesses, contractors, architects, engineers should confirm with the Nevada State Fire Marshal's Office to see if their project falls under the State Fire Marshals statutory authority prior to the start of their project even if approvals have been given by the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ's). The State Fire Marshal's Office would like to see Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ's) add a check box to their plan intake to confirm if the project meets the statutory requirement of the State Fire Marshal's office. Corrective enforcement will begin immediately upon submitting this memorandum via email to the Nevada Contractors Association, Nevada Contractors Board, Nevada Society of Professional Engineers, Nevada Board of Professional Engineers & Land Surveyors, Nevada State Board of Architecture, Interior Design & Residential Design and Authorities Having Jurisdiction, and Nevada State Fire Marshal's Staff.
