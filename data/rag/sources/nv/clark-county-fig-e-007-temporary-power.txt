                                           Clark County Building Department
                                  FIELD INSPECTION GUIDELINE



Division:      Inspections                                                               Policy & Procedure:            FIG-E-007
Subject:       Temporary Electrical Power                                                Effective Date:
Code:         2017NEC Article 590                                                                      Revised Date:                05/08/2024
Proposed:      Matthew Brewer                                                                 Approved:                     Scott Telford

    Policy:
    The  Department  may  allow  utilization of  temporary construction     power  through          the  main  electrical
    building service.  The intent  of this policy   is to provide a power source for the construction     of the  building
    without having     to use a temporary   power pol    e.

    Procedure:
    Procedures and requirements for  temporary power vary  depending     onthe      intended use of  the power and
    the type of occupancy   itserves.    The following is a list of  programs and their  requirements:


    A.   Residential including R2 multi-family housing
          1.   Construction Power Through       Permanent Meter.
              •    The  electrical  panel  must  be  installed  in  accordance     with  Nevada  Power  Standard Design
                   E-105.
              •    The  meter  must  be  approved    after  the footings and foundation have been completed.
              •     The line side of the power distribution system shall be complete. There shall be at least one GFCI
                   protected device connected to the load side.

    B.   Commercial
          1.   Temporary power through the meter.
              •    The electrical meter may be tagged prior to final electrical and/or building inspection approval
                   for construction, equipment and system testing purposes.
              •       Properties that were previously energized and had power removed due to fire, accident, or other
                   hazard causing damage may be authorized for temporary electrical power through the meter for
                   repair, pool maintenance, or landscape maintenance.
              •      Complete Form (#160-        1).
              •    Electrical meter tags issued for all electrical signs, billboards, and cell sites that require a
                   building permit shall not be issued until building final. The electrical tag shall be issued at the
                   time of building final.
