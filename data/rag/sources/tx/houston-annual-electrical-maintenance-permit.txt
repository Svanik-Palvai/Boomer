Annual Electrical Maintenance Permit

Annual Electrical Maintenance Permit

 HPWCODE1048

Permit Description

Upon making written application and payment of all applicable fees, a person having ownership or control over property may obtain an annual maintenance permit for the keeping in safe repair of any and all electrical maintenance work as defined by this code on existing electrical installations, apparatus and equipment in the building and on the property on which the building is located if the applicant employs a person as a full-time employee who will actually perform the electrical maintenance work.

After Approval

Permit must be displayed on premise of property assigned to.

Non-scheduled inspections may be performed to verify property status.

Renewal invoices are mailed out 60 days prior to expiration date.

Conditions & Exceptions

All electrical maintenance work has to be performed by a full-time employee.

Statutory Authority

Texas Occupations Code, Title 8, Chapter 1305 - Electricians

City of Houston Electrical Code Section 301 - Permits and Inspections

Fees

Fee: $272.62 

Admin fee: $33.56 

Renewal fee: $268.88 

Additional Information

Plan Review: Not Required

Inspection: Not Required

Renewal: Required

Valid Period: 1 year

Processing Time: 1 hour in person or up to 48 hours once received by mail
