2026 National Electrical Code Effective September 1, 2026

The 2026 National Electrical Code (NEC) has been adopted by the State of Texas and will become effective September 1, 2026. 

All permit applications submitted on or after the state-mandated effective date will be reviewed for compliance with the new state minimum electrical code.  

For questions, contact Electrical Inspections team at (832) 394-8860.
