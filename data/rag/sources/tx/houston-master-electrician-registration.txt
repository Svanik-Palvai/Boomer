Master Electrician Registration

 HPWCODE1057

Permit Description

Any person or business who wishes to perform electrical work within the City of Houston must register with the Electrical Inspections section. Master Electricians may also register as the Master of Record for an electrical contractor who wishes to do business within the City of Houston.

After Approval

All registered master electricians must be sure to keep documentation of their registration at the job site. 

Conditions & Exceptions

Effective April 1, 2022, per Section 404, The master electrician of record shall not have a city electrical contractor's registration for more than one contracting business. 

Please call Electrical Inspections at 832.394.8860 when the contractor's registration is up for renewal. You will need to indicate which businesses will be dropped at that time.  

Statutory Authority

Texas Occupations Code, Title 8, Chapter 1305 - Electricians

City of Houston Electrical Code Section 301 - Permits and Inspections

Fees

Additional Information

Plan Review: Not Required

Inspection: Not Required

Renewal: Required

Valid Period: 1 year

Processing Time: 1 hour in person or up to 48 hours once received by mail

What You Need

To register as a Master Electrician within the City of Houston, you will need the following documents:

A copy of your Master Electrician’s license granted by the State of Texas,

A copy of your Contractor License granted by the State of Texas, and 

A notarized copy of form CE-1189 Electrical State License Registration and Renewal. 

 Electrical License Registration & Renewal Application (CE1189)

Apply Online

To register online, please send a completed copy of form CE-1189 Electrical State License Registration and Renewal and any supporting documentation to [email protected]

Apply in Person

To register as a Master Electrician, please bring a copy of each of the following to our main office located at 1002 Washington Avenue, Houston, Texas 77002:

A copy of your Master Electrician’s license granted by the State of Texas,

A copy of your Contractor License granted by the State of Texas, and 

A notarized copy of form CE-1189 Electrical State License Registration and Renewal.

Apply by Mail

To register by mail, please send:

A copy of your Master Electrician’s license granted by the State of Texas,

A copy of your Contractor License granted by the State of Texas, and 

A notarized copy of form CE-1189 Electrical State License Registration and Renewal. 

To: P.O. Box 2688, Houston, Texas 77252-2688. 

Apply by Email

To register by fax, please send a completed copy of form CE-1189 Electrical State License Registration and Renewal and any supporting documentation to (832) 395-9601.
