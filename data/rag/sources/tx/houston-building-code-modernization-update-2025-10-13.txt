CODE MODERNIZATION: HISTORY



 The most recent 2021 code adoption was
 performed in 2024. This grant funded program
 aimed to update the City’s dated construction
 codes and modernize its code development
 process.
     • Process spanned almost two years
     • Involved stakeholder engagement, task force
       groups, and committee reviews
     • Hosted internal and external code training
       workshops
     • Successfully adopted the 2021 International
       Building Codes in 2024
