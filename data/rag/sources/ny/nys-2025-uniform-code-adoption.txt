Manufactured Housing Program    Informational Announcement
Adoption of the 2025 Uniform Fire Prevention and Building Code
On July 25, 2025, the State Fire Prevention and Building Code Council (Code Council) adopted rules that
amended and updated the New York State Uniform Fire Prevention and Building Code (Uniform Code) and the
State Energy Conservation Construction Code.

The adopted rules repeal the 2020          edition of the Uniform Code and adopted an amended and updated edition
of a 2025         Uniform Code.   The amended and updated edition of the Uniform Code incorporated, by reference,
several publications including     the 2025 Residential Code of New York State   (publication date: July 2025).
More information on the adoption can be viewed at https://dos.ny.gov/notice -adoption.

The effective date of the 2025         Uniform Code is December 31, 2025.  The 2025 Uniform Code Books can be
viewed for free on the International Code Council (ICC) website at: https://codes.iccsafe.org/codes/united-
states/new-york or purchased at: https://shop.iccsafe.org/custom-codes/state-  codes/new-york.html

What does this mean for the manufactured housing industry in New York State?

Beginning on December 31, 2025, regulated parties submitting building permit applications are required to
comply with the 2025 Uniform Code.

Manufacturers with Design Approval Primary Inspection Agency (D APIA) approved alternative foundation
designs, including     but not limited to, frost-protected shallow foundations that reference the 2020 Residential
Code of New York State (2020 RCNYS) will need to amend and update all     such designs to the 2025
Residential Code of New York State (2025 RCNYS).

Retailers, Installers, and Mechanics shall cease to utilize any foundation design that references the 2020
RCNYS and utilize foundation designs that reference the 2025 RCNYS for all permit applications  and
installations beginning on or after December 31, 2025.  Additionally, local construction     related to the installation
of a manufactured home not contained       within the manufacturer’s installation instructions or addendums
thereto, such as  , porches  /decks and means of egress construction, shall be in accordance with the 2025
RCNYS.  Additions or alterations to existing manufactured homes    not certified under, or designed and
constructed to comply with the applicable provisions    of the Manufactured Housing Construction and Safety
Standards shall be in accordance with the 2025 RCNYS.

Civil   penalties for violations of any rule or regulation adopted by the Department   including provisions of the
Uniform Code       relating to manufactured housing     may be imposed administratively.

Manufactured housing industry personnel needing     technical assistance related to this informational
announcement should email manufactured.housing@dos.ny.gov.  Local authorities having jurisdiction needing
technical assistance related to this information announcement should email codes@dos.ny.gov.
