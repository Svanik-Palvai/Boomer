ELECTRICIAN LICENSES



▪  An Electrician in NYC is a licensed professional
   approved by DOB to perform electrical work in one of
   the five boroughs.

▪  An Applicant can apply for either a Master
   Electrician or Special Electrician.

▪  An Electrician license is valid for 3 years.

▪  There is a business address verification, Business
   Approval and insurance requirement as part of being
   a licensed Electrician.







                                                             4

===== Page 5 =====
ELECTRICIAN LICENSES



▪  Master Electrician: An individual authorized to perform electrical work in or on any building, premises, or
   lot in the city of New York.

        ▪   A Master Electrician is categorized as Responsible Representative OR Designee within a Firm.

        ▪   An authorization number, or Firm Number, will be issued to every Master Electrician business, and
            it shall be included on all applications for Permits and any other documents required to be filed with
            the department.

        ▪   If the business is a partnership or corporation, the name of the responsible representative and the
            names of all other Master Electricians associated with such business must be disclosed to the
            department.







                                                              5

===== Page 6 =====
ELECTRICIAN LICENSES



▪  Special Electrician: An individual authorized to perform electrical work in or on specific buildings to
   perform electrical work under the employment of a building owner, lots or parts thereof in NYC.

         ▪  A Special Electrician is categorized as Responsible Representative OR Designee within a Firm.

         ▪  Special Electrician also has a Civil Service category which is only applicable to them.

         ▪  If an Applicant falls under the Civil Service category, they do not have to take Written or Practical
            exams. Instead, they submit Civil Service Exam report.

         ▪  A Civil Service Electrician will NOT be charged for any license-related functionalities except for the
            Permit fees.

         ▪  Special Electricians can enter in business information, but partners are not required to be disclosed
            as part of that business information.

ELECTRICIAN LICENSES - QUALIFICATIONS


To become an NYC-licensed Electrician, applicants are
required to meet these qualifications:

     ▪  Be at least 18 years old

     ▪  Be able to read and write the English language

     ▪  Be fit to perform the work authorized by the
        license

     ▪  Have good moral character



▪  For more details about becoming an Electrician, see the
DOB Web Site.

===== Page 9 =====
ELECTRICIAN LICENSES - EXAMINATIONS


▪  Electrician Applicants must pass first a Written Examination and then a Practical Examination before
   beginning their application for a new license.

▪  The aspiring licensee must go to the DOB NOW Public Portal to pay for their exam. Once the payment is
   processed, the exam vendor will contact the Applicant to schedule the exam.

▪  For first-time test takers, the Practical Exam must be applied for within 60 days of passing the Written
   Exam.

▪  The fee for the Written Exam is $585 and the fee for the Practical Exam is $530.

ELECTRICIAN LICENSES – BUSINESS APPROVAL


•  To obtain a Master Electrician license, the applicant is required to be an officer/partner/sole
   proprietor of a business/company and the business must have an office in New York City that
   complies with all Zoning Code Regulations.

•  Business Approval Requests (BAR) and Business Address Verification                      will be applicable for all
   Electricians.

•  Electricians also must submit insurance information.

HOW TO REGISTER AND PAY FOR AN EXAM

▪  Electricians must pass both a Written Exam and then a Practical Exam.
▪  To be eligible to take the Written Exam, Applicants cannot have taken the exam in the last                14 days   or
   three or more times in the past six months           .

▪  First-time test takers must apply for the Practical Exam within 60 days of passing the                 Written Exam.

▪  Users who must re-take the Practical Exam:

        ▪   Cannot apply within 90 days of the last exam date

        ▪   Can only re-apply within 24 months of the Written Exam pass date

===== Page 70 =====
RESPONDING TO OBJECTIONS OR REQUESTING RECONSIDERATION


▪  The Background Unit has three options for responses to License Applications:

        ▪  Approved: The background investigation is complete, and the License Application can move
           forward for review by the Licensing Unit.



        ▪  Objections: There is an issue with the License Application that must be addressed by the Applicant
           before the background investigation can be completed.



        ▪  Denied: The License Application cannot be approved with the information given. The Applicant
           can  request to Reconsider the denial.
