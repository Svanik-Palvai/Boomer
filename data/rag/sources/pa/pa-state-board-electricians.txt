State Board of Electrical Licensure

Act 108 of 2026 was signed into law on October 22, 2026, establishing the State Board of Electrical Licensure. The Act provides a two-year period before licensure requirements take effect. Please check this page regularly for updates.

As a reminder, licensing boards and the Bureau of Professional and Occupational Affairs (BPOA) are unable to provide advisory opinions or interpretations of the law.
