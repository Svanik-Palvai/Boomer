Applicable codes 

Beginning July 1, 2026, the City of Philadelphia will adopt the 2021 International Code Council’s (ICC) family of codes with local amendments.

For more details, see our code bulletin about the implementation of the 2021 international codes.

For a complete listing of applicable codes, see the table below.
