## Physical acceptance — team action required

- Serve the teammate-owned Vercel deployment over HTTPS. Check the actual mode label; configuration does not prove connectivity.
- Mount the phone, light the inert prop, use earbuds. Confirm camera frames continue during the call, or use two devices.
- Confirm GPS town and manual correction. Verify a fresh frame, unclear retry, repeat/why/back/stop, both spoken hard gates, and dropped-call recovery.
- Rehearse the LOAD mistake and correction. Complete the job and verify record + requested iMessage delivery; show link fallback if delivery fails.
- Sign in on the capture phone; reviewer binds the worker account. Obtain recording consent. Record the real inert session and retain original call audio and transcript.
- Verify direct upload finalization and all media assets. Start the trusted laptop worker (`scripts/lessons/run.cjs`); FFmpeg must be installed.
- Open the private lesson draft. Select clips, retain mistake/correction context, adjust sync, captions, freeze-frame labels and sources. Render and inspect the entire MP4.
- Record an electrician review with transparent credentials and the worker's publishing permission for the final revision. Any edit requires a new render and approvals.
- Publish and play on Vercel; verify captions, chapters, transcript, sources and region. Test withdrawal with a disposable fixture, never remove real content casually.
- Save a recording of the successful rehearsal as an explicitly labeled backup. Never substitute stock photography or synthetic footage for a completed real session.

## Remaining external dependencies

Real-device capture, provider delivery, electrician review and worker consent cannot be manufactured by an automated repo task. If unavailable, keep the draft private and explicitly mark publication pending. No paid services are activated by this checklist.
